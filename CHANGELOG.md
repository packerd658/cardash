# Changelog

## November 2026 (1.2)

- On car screens with a curved edge, CarDash no longer leaves an extra empty strip beside the curve when the car allows apps to use that space

## November 2026 (1.1)

- Works on aftermarket Android car stereos (head units running Android 12 or newer)
- CarDash can now be set as the home screen on a car stereo, so it opens when the car starts
- The Settings and notification-access buttons no longer crash on devices that don't have those screens

## October 2026 — first release (1.0)

- CarPlay-style dashboard with Google Maps, now playing, weather, calendar, battery and gas price widgets
- YouTube Music screen with Now Playing, Up Next, Search and Library tabs
- MapQuest button and Gemini assistant button
- Apps screen with only car-friendly apps, plus Settings and Exit
- Split-screen support with the dock on the outer edge
- Wallpapers: album art, your photo, or gradient presets
